Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where is Arunyam located?

Arunyam is located at Kondivade A.M., Maval, near Lonavala, surrounded by the beautiful Andra Dam backwaters.

Is Arunyam a RERA registered project?

Yes, Arunyam is a RERA registered project. The RERA number is P52100080147.

What types of villas are available at Arunyam?

Phase 1 offers 45 Private Villas, while Phase 2 offers 18 Courtyard Villas based on a Twin Villa concept.

What is the starting price of Arunyam villas?

Private Villas start from ₹2.42 Cr, while Courtyard Villas start from ₹1.44 Cr, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

What amenities are available at Arunyam?

The project offers a 53,000 sq. ft. clubhouse, forest trail, boating, waterfront promenade, natural waterfall and premium green surroundings.

Is Arunyam suitable for investment?

Yes, Arunyam offers a combination of luxury living, natural surroundings and a location near Lonavala, making it suitable for holiday home buyers as well as long-term property investment.
